ACCOUNT-LESS APPLICATION ARCHITECTURE

1. No User Account Required

SwiftShield VPN is designed as an account-less utility. You do not need to register, provide an email address, create a password, or submit personal profile information to use the Application.

Because there are no user accounts, we do not maintain online account credentials, usernames, passwords, or associated personal databases on our servers.

LOCAL DATA CLEARING

2. How to Delete Locally Stored Data

The Application stores minimal non-sensitive configuration data locally on your device (e.g., your selected VPN protocol UDP/TCP, dark/light theme preference, and server cache). You can completely purge this data at any time through either of the following steps:

Method A: In-App Cache Erasure
  1. Open SwiftShield VPN.
  2. Tap the Settings icon in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Clear Server Cache & Sync. All cached server endpoints and preferences will be immediately deleted.
Method B: Android OS App Storage Erasure
  1. On your Android device, navigate to Settings → Apps → SwiftShield VPN.
  2. Tap Storage & Cache.
  3. Tap Clear Storage and Clear Cache.
  4. All local preferences, timestamps, and cache files will be permanently erased.
Method C: Uninstalling the Application

Uninstalling SwiftShield VPN immediately and permanently removes all application sandbox data, cache, and preferences from your device.

ADVERTISING IDENTIFIER DELETION

3. Resetting or Deleting Google Advertising Identifier (GAID)

The Free version of SwiftShield VPN displays non-intrusive ads using Google AdMob. If you wish to reset or permanently delete the advertising identifier associated with your device:

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads depending on your Android OS version).
  3. Tap Reset advertising ID to create a new randomized ID, or tap Delete advertising ID to permanently opt out of personalized ad tracking across all applications.
