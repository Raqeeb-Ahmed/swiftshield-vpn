OVERVIEW & COMMITMENT

1. Introduction

Welcome to SwiftShield VPN ("we", "our", or "the Application"). We are dedicated to providing secure, encrypted internet connectivity while respecting and safeguarding your personal privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use SwiftShield VPN, our strict no-logging principles, how the Android VpnService is utilized, and how third-party advertising partners function within the free version of the Application.

GOOGLE PLAY VPNSERVICE COMPLIANCE

2. Android VpnService & Encrypted Tunnel

SwiftShield VPN uses Android's native VpnService as its foundational and primary core functionality.

  • Purpose: To establish a device-level secure, encrypted virtual private network tunnel to remote proxy endpoints, protecting your communications on public Wi-Fi and untrusted networks.
  • Encryption: Data transmitted through the tunnel is encrypted end-to-end between your device and the VPN tunnel endpoint using industry-standard protocols (OpenVPN with AES-256 encryption).
  • No Traffic Manipulation: SwiftShield VPN does not modify, intercept, inspect, or inject advertisements into your network traffic, website visits, or other applications.
  • User Consent: Before establishing any tunnel connection, a prominent in-app disclosure is displayed detailing our VPN functionality, requiring your explicit consent before system permissions are requested.
STRICT DATA PROTECTION

3. Our Zero-Logs Policy

We maintain a rigorous zero-logs commitment regarding your private online browsing activity. When connected to SwiftShield VPN:

  • We do not log or store the websites, apps, or online services you access.
  • We do not record your original real IP address during or after your connection session.
  • We do not monitor or record DNS queries made through the encrypted tunnel.
  • We do not log, record, or inspect the contents of your internet traffic.
MONETIZATION & THIRD-PARTY SDKs

4. Advertising in the Free Version (Google AdMob)

SwiftShield VPN is offered as a free-to-use utility supported by non-intrusive in-app advertisements delivered via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK.

To serve advertisements and detect fraud, Google AdMob may process certain technical data as permitted by its privacy policy:

  • Ephemeral Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) used by Google for ad attribution and frequency capping.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Ad load times, error logs, and performance metrics collected by Google Play Services.

Ads are rendered strictly inside SwiftShield VPN's own user interface (banner and post-disconnect interstitials). We never monetize third-party traffic or inject ads into third-party websites or external apps.

For more details on Google's privacy practices, please visit Google Privacy Policy and How Google Uses Data in Advertising.

PERMISSIONS

5. Device Permissions Required

The Application requests only the minimum necessary Android permissions to function:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Essential for internet communication and establishing the VPN handshake.
  • android.permission.BIND_VPN_SERVICE: Core permission required by the Android OS to route network packets through the secure tunnel.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Required to maintain the VPN connection alive in the background without being killed by OS battery optimizations.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional permission on Android 13+ to display the active VPN status and live session timer in your status bar.
